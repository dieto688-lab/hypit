# Brief — Bomba del Zar en la Fosa de las Marianas

**Goal:** a vertical 9:16 explainer video (~2 min) answering: what would happen
if the Tsar Bomba (50 Mt) detonated at the bottom of the Mariana Trench?
The counterintuitive answer: the ocean absorbs almost everything — no tsunami,
no mushroom cloud — while a smaller bomb on an island (Castle Bravo) was far
worse. Closing line: water is the planet's best shield.

**Supplied material:** full Spanish script with per-segment tone/audio notes
and ten scene prompts (vertical 9:16, minimalist vector motion-graphics style).
Narration was generated from that script (7 segments, Spanish male voice).

**Constraints:**
- 1080×1920, 30 fps, Spanish narration + phrase captions.
- No paid generation services: all visuals are authored HTML/CSS/SVG/canvas
  components; music and effects are synthesized locally.
- Captions are phrase-level (authored time), not word-level karaoke: no speech
  alignment service is configured in this environment.

**Agreed scope:** one finished MP4 (`final.video`) plus the editable Hypit
project (Sources, component, runtime) so the piece can be revised and re-rendered.
